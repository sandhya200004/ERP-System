## 🎯 What Each Role Can See

### **CEO / CTO / CMO** (You & Founders)
✅ Full access to everything
- Dashboard with analytics
- All customer & financial data
- All employee KPIs, attendance, and tasks
- Reports and settings
- Can manage users and company settings

### **HR & Managers**
✅ Management features
- Dashboard with team analytics
- Customer management
- Invoices, payments, quotes
- All employee KPIs and attendance
- Reports
- Cannot access company settings

### **Developers & Designers**
✅ Limited access
- Own profile
- Own attendance
- Own KPI dashboard (can log daily tasks)
- Cannot see other employees' data
- Cannot see financial information

### **Marketing Team**
✅ Customer-facing features
- Customer management
- Leads & prospects
- Quotes & proposals
- Own profile, attendance, KPI
- Cannot see invoices/payments
- Cannot see other employees' data

### **R&D Team**
✅ Basic access
- Own profile
- Own attendance
- Own KPI dashboard
- Cannot see business data
- Cannot see other employees' data
